## Scope / timing / orthography observations (indexing)
- Decimal orthography: EN uses point (`0.5 ml`); DE uses comma (`0,5 ml`) at corresponding dose loci.
- Ratio punctuation: EN `1:1,000`; DE often `1:1.000` / `1:1.000` in extracts.
- Timing windows for hypersensitivity (where present in V10 acute sections) use minutes/hours language in both; exact phrases retained in indexes.
- No preference stated between EN and DE when wording, section numbers, or packing differ.
